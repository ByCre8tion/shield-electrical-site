# Shield photos

Drop real job photos in this folder and the site updates itself in about a minute.

## How to add a photo

1. Open https://github.com/ByCre8tion/shield-electrical-site/upload/main/photos
2. Rename the photo on your computer to the **slot name** from the table below, for example `field1.jpg`.
3. Drag it into the page and press **Commit changes**.
4. Wait about a minute, then refresh https://shield-electrical-az.web.app

To replace a photo, upload a new file with the same name. To remove one, open the file here and delete it.

**Rules**
- JPG, PNG or WebP. iPhone HEIC photos: export or share them as JPG first.
- The name must match a slot exactly. `field1.jpg` works, `Field 1.jpg` does not.
- Under 25 MB each. Shoot wide; the site crops to fit the frame.
- Real Shield jobs and people only. No stock or AI images.

Every empty frame on the site also shows its slot name, like `Add photos/field1.jpg`.
